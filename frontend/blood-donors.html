Blood Type Compatibility

Quick Guide

Knowing which blood types are compatible is critical during emergency transfusions. Match patient requirements with compatible donor groups below:

Universal Red Cell Donor O-

Can give red blood cells to any of the 8 blood types.

Universal Recipient AB+

Can safely receive red blood cells from any blood type.

Donation Interval 90 Days

Minimum rest period required between whole blood donations.
